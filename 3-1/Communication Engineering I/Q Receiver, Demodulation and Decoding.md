4. **(p. 5, (c) [Rx2] — Topic-Wise PYQ, Series 21)** Explain how the use of an RF amplifier improves the SNR of a super-heterodyne receiver.

   (ii) Determine the bandwidth (BW) of the receiver at $550$ and $1550\text{ kHz}$.
   (iii) Will the receiver face any problem while turning to these two particular frequencies? Investigate.
