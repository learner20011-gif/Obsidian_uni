# Perfectly Fluffy Japanese Fried Rice (Yakimeshi)

## 📐 The Core Ratio
*What is the mathematical foundation of this dish?*
- **Ratio:** 1 part egg to 1 part cooked rice (approximate)
- **Base Yield:** 1-2 Servings

## 🧠 The "Why" (Theory & Technique)
*Why does this recipe work? What is the core technique?*
- **Primary Technique:** [[Wok Hei]], [[High Heat Frying]]
- **The Science/Purpose:** Pre-coating cold rice grains with egg wash separates them. Cooking on high heat evaporates excess moisture rapidly, preventing the rice from becoming soggy and sticking together, resulting in a fluffy texture.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
*Where does this usually go wrong?*
- **Mistake 1:** Using freshly cooked, warm rice (too much moisture).
- **Mistake 2:** Overcrowding the pan or wok, lowering the temperature and steaming the rice instead of frying.
- **Mistake 3:** Not tossing and breaking up the clumps quickly.

## 🥣 Mise en Place (Ingredients)
*List ingredients in the order they are used. Group them by phase if helpful.*
- [ ] 2 cups Cooked short-grain Japanese rice (chilled or day-old)
- [ ] 2 Eggs (beaten)
- [ ] 2 tbsp Oil (neutral or sesame)
- [ ] 1/2 cup Chashu pork or bacon (diced)
- [ ] 1/4 cup Green onions/scallions (chopped)
- [ ] 1 clove Garlic (minced, optional)
- [ ] 1 tbsp Soy sauce
- [ ] Salt and Black Pepper to taste

## 🍳 The Process (Chunked)
*Break the recipe down into 3-4 major phases, not 20 micro-steps.*

### Phase 1: Prep & Base
- Chop chashu/bacon and green onions. Beat the eggs.
- (Optional Cheat) Coat the chilled rice with half of the beaten egg mixture to separate grains beforehand.

### Phase 2: The Main Cook
- Heat pan or wok over very high heat until smoking, then add oil.
- Pour in the beaten eggs and immediately add the rice before the eggs fully set.
- Quickly toss and press the rice with a spatula/ladle to break apart clumps.
- Add diced chashu/protein and keep tossing.

### Phase 3: Finishing & Adjusting
- Season with salt and black pepper.
- Drizzle soy sauce around the edges of the pan (not directly on the rice) to caramelize it slightly for flavor.
- Toss in chopped green onions at the very end.
- Taste for seasoning and serve immediately.

---
## 🔄 Variations & Substitutions
- **If missing X, use Y:** Use bacon, spam, or shrimp instead of chashu.
- **Flavor twist:** Add a dash of sesame oil or white pepper at the end.
